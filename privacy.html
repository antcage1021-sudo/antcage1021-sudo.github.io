Privacy Policy for Frame

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Information Accessed

Frame uses Google OAuth 2.0 to request read-only access to the signed-in user's YouTube channel list, uploaded video metadata, and YouTube Analytics audience retention reports (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly).

2. How Data Is Used and Stored

Frame runs locally on the user's own computer. OAuth tokens and fetched retention reports are stored strictly locally on the user's device so the user can review where viewers drop off or rewatch their own videos. Frame does not operate a remote backend, does not collect analytics on users, and does not sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to any third party.

3. Revoking Access & Deleting Data

You can revoke Frame's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, and delete local tokens and cached reports directly from your computer.

4. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact intelarthur5@gmail.com.
